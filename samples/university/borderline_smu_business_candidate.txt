small organization actually manages money rather than just reading about it in an Economics textbook.

decisions later. My weekend job at a cafe taught me a similar lesson from the other side of the counter:
how a business balances labor cost against customer experience during a rush. I am applying to SMU's
Business Administration program because I want to formalize this intuition with real frameworks in
finance, strategy and organizational behavior, and because SMU's interview-based admissions and
seminar-style teaching match how I like to learn — by discussing and defending an argument, not just
memorizing it.
