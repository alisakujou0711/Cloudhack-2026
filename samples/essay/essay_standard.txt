Essay Question 1: Why do you want to study Computer Science at NUS?
--------------------------------------------------
My interest in Computer Science started in secondary school when I joined my school's robotics
club and helped write the control code for our competition robot. Since then I have taught
myself the basics of Python and built a few small projects, including a to-do list app and a
simple quiz game for my friends. I want to study Computer Science at NUS because I know it has
a strong reputation in Southeast Asia and offers a wide range of modules covering both software
engineering and artificial intelligence, which are areas I am curious about. I believe NUS will
give me the technical foundation and the exposure to research that I need to grow from someone
who tinkers with code into someone who can build real systems.

Essay Question 2: Describe a challenge you have overcome.
--------------------------------------------------
In my second year of junior college, I was made the leader of a group project to build a simple
website for our school's recycling initiative, even though I had never led a team before. Two
of my teammates disagreed on which framework to use, and the project stalled for almost a week.
I organized a meeting where each person explained their reasoning, and we eventually agreed on
a simpler approach that let everyone contribute. We finished the website a few days before the
deadline and it is still used by the school today. This experience taught me that leadership is
less about having all the answers and more about creating space for the team to reach one.
